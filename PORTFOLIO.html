About — 03

Studying interactive graphic design —
building with raw matter.

I'm Elmahdi Boukhaira (AM7), a 4th-year Interactive Graphic Design student at ENSAD. My work is anchored in raw, brutal aesthetics — I push against the polished and the decorative.

Daily practice: 3D modeling in Blender, 3D printing, acrylic and mixed-media collages, and packaging design that experiments with structure, matter and contradiction — with a constant attention to UI/UX principles.
